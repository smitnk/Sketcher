# B16 - subject-aware AI pipeline

Files: `SubjectCrop.kt` (new), `SketchPipeline.kt`, `CartoonLineExtractor.cartoonScore`.

- `SubjectCrop.detect` strips letterbox bars (phone-gallery screenshots incl. status-bar glyphs), then finds a flat/graded
  backdrop connected to the border and returns the subject rect (+6%) and a background mask. Photos -> full frame.
- `cartoonScore` is measured on the subject only and requires dark outlines on strong edges; 3D renders/photos on flat
  backdrops no longer score as cartoon (elf: 0.38).
- AI path: the model runs on the subject crop taken from the ORIGINAL image, resampled to long side 768 (1024 when
  detail > 0.7); strokes are mapped back to working-image coordinates. Ink in the background mask and strokes along the
  frame are dropped.
